常见问题

应用里有自带的影视内容或片源吗？

没有。MeowTV 不内置任何视频内容或播放源，需要您连接自己部署的 MeowTV 服务器才能使用。

登录时需要提供什么信息？

登录需要两个信息：您部署的 MeowTV 服务器地址（例如 http://192.168.1.10:8080），以及在该服务器上创建好的账号与密码。

默认播放为什么占用很多流量 / 存储？

客户端默认采用较为激进的缓冲策略，以换取更流畅的拖动和切换体验。如果您介意流量或本地存储占用，可以在应用的播放设置中将模式切换为 HLS（按需切片加载）。

投屏 / 远程播放需要什么条件？

投屏依赖 DLNA、AirPlay 或 Chromecast 等协议。iOS 设备首次投屏时，系统会询问是否允许"本地网络"权限，请选择允许；同时确保您的手机与投屏设备（如电视、盒子）处于同一 Wi-Fi 网络下。

About MeowTV

MeowTV is a media client that connects to a self-hosted MeowTV server. It lets you browse, play, favorite, and manage your own media library. The app does not include any built-in video content or sources, and does not provide any unauthorized media content.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app include any built-in media content or sources?

No. MeowTV does not bundle any video content or playback sources. You need to connect it to a MeowTV server that you deploy yourself.

What do I need to sign in?

You need two things: the address of the MeowTV server you deployed (for example, http://192.168.1.10:8080), and the account credentials you created on that server.

Why does playback use a lot of bandwidth or storage by default?

By default the client uses an aggressive buffering strategy to deliver smoother seeking and switching. If you prefer to save bandwidth or local storage, switch the playback mode to HLS (on-demand segmented streaming) in the app's playback settings.

What do I need for casting or remote playback?

Casting relies on DLNA, AirPlay, or Chromecast. On iOS the first time you cast, the system will ask for permission to access the "Local Network" — please allow it. Make sure your phone and the target device (TV, set-top box, etc.) are on the same Wi-Fi network.

How can I send feedback or report an issue?

Please email us at meowycn@163.com with your device model, OS version, a description of the issue, and any relevant screenshots. We will reply as soon as possible on business days.
